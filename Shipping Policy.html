Shipping Policy

I ship all domestic orders using Pirate Ship, which lets me access discounted carrier rates and pass those savings on to you. This is why I ask for your full name and mailing address in your order email, so I can generate an accurate shipping label and rate for your piece.

For international orders, please email me directly to arrange shipping, as rates and delivery times vary by destination.

Each piece is carefully packed to prevent breakage in transit. Orders typically ship within 5–7 business days of payment.

All Sales Final

Due to the handmade nature of each piece, all sales are final. I'm unable to offer refunds or exchanges for change of mind.

Damages

If your piece arrives damaged, please contact me as soon as possible with photos of the damage and packaging. We will find a way to make it right.
